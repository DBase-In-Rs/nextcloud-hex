# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful and practical place for collaboration.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally, stay focused on the work, and avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Discuss technical disagreements with concrete reasoning.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when something breaks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or threats.
- Personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Publishing private security reports before maintainers have had time to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users from participating if needed.
